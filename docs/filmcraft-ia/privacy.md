# FilmCraft IA — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

FilmCraft IA is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's computer. It has no server, no
analytics and no advertising.

**Data used.** When the owner connects their TikTok or YouTube account, the tool receives an access
token and basic profile information (TikTok: open id, display name, avatar; YouTube: channel name).
It uses them only to upload the owner's own video clips and to show which account is connected.

**Where it is stored.** Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local folder. Nothing is
sent anywhere except to TikTok and Google, through their official APIs, to publish the clips.

**Sharing.** No data is sold or shared with anyone.

**Deletion.** Disconnecting the app in the TikTok or Google account settings revokes access.
Deleting the local folder deletes all stored tokens.

**Children.** The tool is not directed at children.

Contact: open an issue on this repository.
